<p>Make a difference in your community - become a Grassroots Leader (GRL)
today! As a GRL, you'll be the vital link between residents and the government,
helping to build a more cohesive and caring society. You'll have the opportunity
to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>Organise community events and programmes</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Assist neighbours in need</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Gather feedback on local issues</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Contribute to policy discussions</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Join us in creating a stronger, more connected Singapore. Your involvement
can make a real impact on the lives of those around you.</p>
