<h3>Join Us in Making a Difference in Our Community!</h3>
<p>Are you looking for a way to give back and help your neighbors? We’re
inviting passionate and dedicated residents to become volunteers and play
a key role in creating a vibrant, supportive community. Whether you have
a few hours to spare or want to take on a more involved role, there’s a
place for you!</p>

<h4>Why Volunteer?</h4>
<p>Make a Positive Impact: Your efforts will directly contribute to improving
the lives of those around you.
<br>Build Connections: Meet new people and build stronger bonds with your
neighbors.
<br>Develop New Skills: Gain valuable experience in leadership, organisation,
and problem-solving.
<br>Give Back: Volunteering is a rewarding way to support the community that
supports you.
<br>How Can You Help? We have a variety of volunteer opportunities, from organising
local events to helping with community programs, and more. No matter your
interests or availability, there’s a role for you!
<br>Ready to make a difference? Join us and be part of something special!
<br>Together, we can create a stronger, more connected community!</p>
<p>Click <a href="https://go.gov.sg/tjcvo" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">here</a> to
become a community volunteer today.</p>
