<p>Birdwatching sessions will be organized monthly for residents, focusing
on biodiversity and conservation efforts. These guided sessions will provide
nature-related activities for everyone to enjoy, creating an opportunity
to come together, make new friends, and connect with nature.</p>

Gardens and observe the stunning variety of birds in their natural habitat.</p>
