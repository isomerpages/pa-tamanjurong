<p>Birdwatching sessions will be organized monthly for residents, focusing
on biodiversity and conservation efforts. These guided sessions will provide
nature-related activities for everyone to enjoy, creating an opportunity
to come together, make new friends, and connect with nature.</p>

<p>Location: Jurong Lake Gardens
<br>Time: 8 AM - 9:30 AM</p>
<p>Visit <a href="http://go.gov.sg/tjbirdwatching2024" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank"><u>go.gov.sg/tjbirdwatching2024</u></a><u> </u>to
register your interest in joining us for a day of discovery at Jurong Lake
Gardens and observe the stunning variety of birds in their natural habitat.
Do follow us on <a href="https://www.facebook.com/tjgreenatheart" rel="noopener nofollow" target="_blank">Facebook</a> for
the updates on the next session!</p>
