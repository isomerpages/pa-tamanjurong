<p>Count Me In is an innovative programme designed to support K2 children
who are experiencing challenges in learning mathematics. Organised annually
by the Taman Jurong Youth Network, this initiative aims to cultivate a
genuine interest in maths among young learners while providing essential
guidance through their educational journey.</p>
<p>At the heart of Count Me In are our dedicated Youth Network volunteers.
These enthusiastic individuals work closely with K2 children, employing
creative and engaging methods to make mathematics more accessible and enjoyable.
Our volunteers not only teach mathematical concepts but also strive to
instil a love for numbers and problem-solving in these young minds.</p>

<p>We warmly welcome individuals who have a passion for teaching mathematics
to join our initiative. Whether you're a seasoned educator or simply someone
who enjoys working with children and has a knack for numbers, your contribution
can make a significant difference in a child's learning experience.</p>
