<p>Juboon Dragon and Lion Dance is a dynamic interest group at Taman Jurong
Community Club, boasting a rich history of performances spanning four decades.
Our troupe has consistently showcased its skills and passion for this traditional
art form, actively competing in various national and international competitions.</p>
<p>We warmly welcome new members aged 6 and above, embracing individuals
from all races and religions. Whether you're an experienced performer or
a complete novice, Juboon opens its doors to you.</p>
<p>Our comprehensive training programme goes beyond teaching the intricacies
of dragon and lion dance. While you'll gain in-depth knowledge and skills
in this cultural art, you'll also benefit from physical conditioning that
enhances overall fitness.</p>
<p>Join Juboon to experience the thrill of dragon and lion dance, forge new
friendships, immerse yourself in a vibrant cultural tradition, and improve
your physical fitness. Become part of our Juboon family and embark on an
exciting journey that combines cultural exploration with personal growth!</p>
