<h4>Balloon Sculpting IG</h4>
<p>The Balloon Sculpting Interest Group is a dynamic community dedicated
to the art of balloon sculpting. This group brings together individuals
of all skill levels, from seasoned professionals to beginners eager to
explore this creative craft. Its primary mission is to celebrate the beauty
of balloon art, provide a space for learning, and inspire members to create
unique and captivating designs.</p>
<p>For more information, please contact Taman Jurong CC at 62651711.</p>
