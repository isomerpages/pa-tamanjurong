<h4>Eco-Upcycling Interest Group</h4>
<p>At Eco-Upcycling, we believe in the power of creativity to make a difference.
Our mission is to breathe new life into materials that are often overlooked
or discarded, transforming them into functional, and eco-friendly products.
The interest group focus on repurposing materials that typically go unused—such
as coffee sachets—into unique products that could be used.</p>
<p>One of the signature creations is the upcycled coffee sachet bags, where
the interest group take what is typically seen as waste and craft it into
chic, durable, and one-of-a-kind fashion pieces.</p>
<p>Join us in rethinking the possibilities of everyday materials—because
with Eco-Upcycling, waste is just the beginning of something beautiful.</p>
