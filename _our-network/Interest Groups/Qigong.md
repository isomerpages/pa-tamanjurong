<h4>Haircutting</h4>
<p>The Haircutting Interest Group is an initiative by the Taman Jurong CC
Women’s Executive Committee (WEC), designed to support residents facing
financial difficulties by offering free professional haircutting services.
This initiative aims to assist individuals who may struggle to access basic
grooming services.</p>
<p>The group is committed to making a positive impact on the lives of local
community members through this thoughtful and meaningful service. Taman
Jurong residents who wish to benefit from this initiative are encouraged
to contact the Taman Jurong Community Centre at 6265 1711 for more information,
as there are limited slots available</p>
