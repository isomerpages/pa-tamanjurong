<h4>Line Dance </h4>
<p>The Line Dance interest group dedicated to empowering women through the
joy of line dancing. With a passion for rhythm, movement, and camaraderie,
this group offers a supportive environment where women of all ages and
skill levels can come together to explore the art of line dancing. Whether
they’re beginners or seasoned dancers, members can enjoy weekly lessons
designed to improve their technique, boost their confidence, and build
lasting friendships. Through line dance, the group fosters physical fitness,
mental well-being, and a sense of community, making it the perfect space
for women to have fun, learn, and connect with others who share their enthusiasm.</p>
