<p>The Taman Jurong Community Club Indian Activity Executive Committee (IAEC)
plays a vital role in fostering social cohesion among Taman Jurong residents,
particularly within the Indian community while also promoting racial harmony
with other groups. The IAEC organizes various events and activities for
Taman Jurong residents, encouraging mutual respect and strengthen relationships
between Indians and other communities. This page, brought to you by Taman
Jurong IAEC, serves as a resource for residents to stay informed about
upcoming Indian activities and events, enhancing community engagement and
celebrating the rich diversity of our neighbourhood.</p>
