<p>The Taman Jurong Community Sports Network (CSN) is committed to fostering
community bonding through a diverse range of sports activities that cater
to residents of all ages. Regular interest groups and activities are organised,
providing opportunities to engage in sports such as Basketball, Football,
Volleyball, and Pickleball, with sessions tailored for the residents. Additionally,
the Taman Jurong CSN organises a variety of events, including Sports Competitions
and Inter-Divisional Games, where residents come together to represent
their community and strengthen the spirit of unity.</p>
