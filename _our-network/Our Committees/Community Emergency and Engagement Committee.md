<p>The Taman Jurong Community Emergency and Engagement (C2E) Committee helps
strengthen community resilience and cohesion among our Taman Jurong residents
and stakeholders.</p>
<p>During peacetime, our Committee develops and maintains the Community EP
plans, structures and systems by organising exercises and EP-centric activities
to stay prepared while ensuring operational readiness during emergencies.
We also partner with various government emergency authorities to educate
the public on what to do during an emergency.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">

<p>In the unlikely event of a crisis, we coordinate grassroots’ emergency
response efforts through our vital partnerships and community resources,
supporting emergency authorities in defusing tension and restoring public
confidence and normalcy through the dissemination of critical information
and feedback collection.</p>
<p>Under the purview of our Committee, we have a central and organised pool
of passionate and dedicated Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) volunteers.
Our volunteers are equipped with specialised training to respond to emergencies
and assist the community in the recovery process. They will undergo provider
certification training in CPR &amp; AED as well as Standard First Aid and
other related courses to enable them to carry out duties in times of activation.
During peacetime, our CERT members are typically deployed to support National-level
events such as Chingay, community events and other ad-hoc activities that
require safety coverage and incident response.</p>
<p>Working in close collaboration with our volunteers, partners and residents,
our team has consistently recognised its efforts in various key areas of
EP and community safety. This includes receiving the Total Defence (TD)
Advocate Award for Organisations, PA-SCDF CERT Award for consecutive years,
National Civil Emergency Preparedness (NCEP) Award for Platinum Tier, Municipal
Services Award, SCDF's Community First Responder and Life Saver Awards,
and recently achieved runner-up for Singapore-Global Firefighters and Paramedics
Challenge in 2023. These awards and accolades are a testament to our collective
resolve, efforts and belief that we can and have much to contribute to
our community's safety, cohesion and resilience and our volunteers and
partners are the bedrock that made these achievements possible.</p>
