<p>Join us for an exciting community event at Taman Jurong! This unique garage
day offers residents a chance to set up mini booths to trade or give away
items. With 70% of booths hosted by residents, you'll find a diverse array
of offerings catering to various interests.</p>
<p>Don't miss out on this opportunity to connect with your neighbours and
perhaps find some hidden treasures. Visit our website and social media
channels to find out the exact location. See you there!</p>
