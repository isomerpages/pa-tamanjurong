<p>Join us for an exciting community event at Taman Jurong! This unique garage
day offers residents a chance to set up mini booths to trade or give away
items. With 70% of booths hosted by residents, you'll find a diverse array
of offerings catering to various interests. Discover workshops for youths,
young adults, and families, making it a perfect outing for everyone.</p>
<p>Don't miss out on this opportunity to connect with your neighbours and
perhaps find some hidden treasures. Follow us on Facebook for updates on
the event date and timing. Visit our website and social media channels
to find out the exact location and other details. See you there!</p>
